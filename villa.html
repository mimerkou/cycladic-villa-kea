03 · Details

Made by hand, kept simple.

Whitewash and blue shutters, stone underfoot, hand-painted plates on the walls. Small things, chosen with care.

04 · Terraces

Terraces on every side, the Aegean in front.

The terraces wrap around the house, so there is always a spot in the sun and another in the shade. The main one sits under the pergola and looks straight out to the Aegean and the sunset.

Breakfast outside, a long lunch at the table, a glass of wine as the sun goes down: the house is made to be lived out here.

05 · The estate

Your own land, among the oaks.

4,000 m² of land with oak trees

The house stands on an estate of 4,000 m² planted with oak trees, which keeps it private and quiet above the village. There is private parking for two cars.

07 · Practical

Good to know before you come.

The details below are indicative. Nicole and her family confirm everything personally when they reply to your request.

01Arrival and departure

Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00. If your ferry times are different, tell us and we will try to make it work.

02Minimum stay

Three nights for most of the year, a week in July and August.

03Price

The total depends on your dates and the number of guests. We send it personally with our reply.

04House rules

No smoking indoors and no parties. Please respect the quiet of the neighbourhood after 23:00.

05Getting here

Kea is reached by ferry from Lavrio. Directions to the villa are on the Location page.
