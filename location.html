Location

Above Ioulida, at the heart of Kea.

Kea is the closest of the Cyclades to Athens, yet it keeps its own slow pace. The villa sits just above Ioulida, the island’s whitewashed capital, looking out over the hills to the Aegean.

  • ≈1 hby ferry from Lavrio
  • Ioulidajust below the villa
  • ≈15′by car from the port

01 · Getting here

From the harbour to the house.

Scroll on and follow the way: across the water to Korissia, then up the hill to Ioulida.

Follow the way on the map: across the water to Korissia, then up the hill to Ioulida.

Step 1 · By sea

Lavrio to Korissia

Ferries leave from Lavrio, about an hour’s drive from Athens and close to the airport. The crossing takes about an hour. In summer, book your tickets early, especially if you bring a car.

Step 2 · By road

Up to Ioulida

From the port, the road climbs inland to Ioulida in about fifteen minutes. A car makes island life easy; there are also taxis at the port, and cars can be rented on Kea.

Step 3 · Arrival

The last stretch

The villa is just above the village, with private parking for two cars. Before you arrive, we send you directions and the exact location, and we are a phone call away.

03 · Discover Kea

Slow days on Kea.

Old stone paths between oak trees, quiet bays and long lunches by the water. A few ideas to start with; ask us for more.

01 / 04

Ioulida

Steep lanes, steps and whitewashed walls, small squares with cafés and the old town hall. Walk down in the morning for bread and coffee, come back in the evening when the village fills with life.

Just below the villa · on foot · morning or evening

The Lion of Kea

A great stone lion carved into the rock more than two thousand years ago, a short walk from Ioulida along an old path. Go early, when the light is soft and the path is cool.
