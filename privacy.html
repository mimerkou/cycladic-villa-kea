01Who we are

The person responsible for your details (the “controller”) is [full name of the owner or company name], [street address], 840 02 Ioulida, Kea [confirm postcode], Greece. Tax ID (AFM) [if required], Property Registry Number (AMA) [number].

For anything about your privacy: [email, e.g. info@cycladicvillakea.com] · +30 694 554 1114.

02What we collect

  • From the request form: arrival and departure dates, number of guests, name, email and, if you wish, phone and message.
  • From our conversations: whatever you write to us or tell us by phone or email.
  • If you book a stay: the details needed for the booking, the payment and the receipt or invoice, as the law requires [to confirm with the accountant].
  • Technical data: like every website, the site’s server briefly records basic browsing data (e.g. IP address, type of device), for security and to keep the site running.

We do not ask for, and please do not send us through the form, sensitive information such as health details.

03Why we use them

  • To reply to your request with availability and the price, and to arrange your stay together. We do this because you asked us to, before a possible booking (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To meet our legal obligations, e.g. tax obligations, when a booking is made (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • To keep the site secure and working properly, e.g. to stop spam (Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interest).

We do not send newsletters or advertising, and we do not sell your details.

04Who can see them

Only we see your details. To run the site we work with a few providers, who process data only on our behalf:

  • hosting of the site: [provider, e.g. the WordPress hosting company]
  • sending the form to our email: [form service]
  • email: [email provider]
  • when a booking is made: our accountant and, where the law requires it, the competent authorities (e.g. tax authorities)

If a provider stores data outside the European Union, we only use providers with the safeguards the GDPR requires (e.g. standard contractual clauses).

05How long we keep them

  • A request that did not lead to a booking: we delete it within [12] months.
  • A booking: we keep the details of the stay and the payment for as long as tax law requires [period, to confirm with the accountant], and then delete them.

06Cookies and site technology

The site does not use cookies for advertising or statistics. It only remembers the language you chose (English or Greek), and only while the browser tab is open. This is needed for the site to work and does not identify you. [If statistics, a map or other third-party services are added, this section changes and consent is needed.]

The links to Instagram and Google Maps take you to third-party websites, with their own privacy policies.

07Your rights

You can ask us at any time:

  • to see which details we hold about you and get a copy
  • to correct or delete them
  • to restrict their use or object to it
  • to give them to you in a format you can take elsewhere
  • to withdraw a consent you gave us

Write to us at [email]. We reply as soon as we can, and in any case within one month.

08If something is not right

We will be glad to sort it out with you. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, 1–3 Kifisias Ave., 115 23 Athens, www.dpa.gr, contact@dpa.gr.

09Changes to this page

If anything changes in the way we use your details, we will update this page and the date at the top.
